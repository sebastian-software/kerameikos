## The template

A prompt has four parts: the reference line, the style, the composition, and the motif with
its accent. Style and composition stay almost the same; the motif paragraph is where the work
is.

### A figure with an object

```
Using the attached emblems as the exact style reference (same line quality,
same level of detail, same colors, same ring), draw one new emblem for the
same product family.

Style: flat vector emblem in the manner of ancient Greek coin and
black-figure vase art. Large deep navy (#0E2A4D) shapes with a strong
silhouette, richly articulated inside by fine cream (#FAF9F4) lines cut
into the navy. Skin (face, neck, arms, hands) is cream, drawn with fine
navy contour lines; hair and garments are navy. One bronze (#BC8A45)
accent. No gradients, no shading, no text. It should look engraved, with
hand-drawn, slightly irregular curves, not like a pictogram or app icon.

Composition: a circular medallion with a bronze ring and an empty cream
ring inside it that the motif never touches. The motif ends a ring's width
inside the bronze ring. The figure is cut by the circle at the bottom,
like a bust on a coin, where the garment forms a broad navy base.
Everything else stands free, with open cream space around the head and
around the object. No pedestal, no ground, no background objects. Square
image, plain cream background.

Motif: <who it is, in one sentence>. <Half-figure in profile facing right,
cut by the circle at the waist: hair, garment, expression>. <What the hands
do, where the object is, how large it is in relation to the figure>. <What
must not appear: the objects of the other marks>. Accent: <the parts> in
bronze. Nothing else is bronze.
```

### A scene without a figure

Replace the skin sentence and the composition paragraph:

```
Composition: a circular medallion with a bronze ring and an empty cream
ring inside it that the motif never touches. The ground at the bottom is
one broad navy mass that is cut by the circle. Everything above it stands
free, with open cream space on both sides and above. No pedestal, no stone
blocks, no human figures, no background objects. Square image, plain cream
background.
```

For a scene with several things in it, add to the style paragraph:

```
Strict side view: everything is shown in flat profile on one ground line,
with no perspective, no foreshortening, and no view from above.
```

## Motif paragraphs that worked

### Stentaro, first try

```
Motif: Stentor, the herald of the Iliad, sounding a salpinx. A male bust
in profile facing right, cut by the circle at the chest like a bust on a
coin, head tilted slightly back, short curly hair held by a plain headband,
cheeks filled with breath. One raised hand holds a long, slender, straight
salpinx trumpet to his lips; it runs diagonally to the upper right and ends
in a small flared bell. A cloak is fastened at the shoulder, drawn with
flowing cream fold lines. No laurel wreath, no helmet. Accent: the entire
trumpet in bronze.
```

### Gramano, first try

The first result had the whole scroll in bronze; the sheet was later made paper-colored on the
tracing masks.

```
Motif: Clio, the Greek muse of history and keeper of records. A single
female half-figure in profile facing right, cut by the circle at the
waist, her head bowed slightly as she reads. Her hair is gathered in a
knot at the back of the head. She wears a draped chiton fastened at the
shoulder, falling in flowing folds. Both hands hold a half-unrolled
papyrus scroll in front of her at chest height: one rolled end in each
hand, the open sheet between them carrying a few fine lines of writing.
The scroll is large, about a third of the medallion's width, and clearly
the thing she is looking at. No laurel wreath, no trumpet, no wings.
Accent: the scroll in bronze, with its lines of writing in navy.
```

### Terminaro, after four other metaphors

```
Motif: the ancient Greek dexiosis as on Attic reliefs: two people sealing
an agreement with a handshake. Two half-figures in profile face each other,
a man on the left and a woman on the right, both cut by the circle at the
waist. Their right hands meet in a firm clasp in the center of the
medallion, at chest height, clearly visible between them. The man has
short curly hair and a cloak over one shoulder; the woman has her hair
gathered in a knot and wears a draped chiton. The two heads are level,
with open cream space between and above them. Keep the faces simple and
calm. No wings, no laurel, no objects. Accent: a bronze bracelet on each
of the two clasped wrists, nothing else in bronze.
```

Later edits made the hands and forearms paper-colored, separated the two garments so that the
figures touch only at the hands, added bronze shoulder clasps and a hair ribbon, and gave the
man a broader body.

### Curano, the version that held

The scene was reached through an olive tree and two attempts at the Ship of Theseus.

```
Motif: a Greek wheelwright keeping a chariot wheel in repair. A strong man
as a half-figure in profile facing right, cut by the circle at the waist:
a short work tunic fastened at one shoulder, bare arms. In front of him,
on the right, stands a large ancient Greek chariot wheel seen flat from
the side: a sturdy rim, a round hub, and four straight spokes in a cross.
With one hand he holds the rim; with the other he fits one new spoke into
place between hub and rim. Three spokes are navy like the rest of the
wheel. Accent: the one new spoke in bronze, nothing else in bronze.
```

Then, as an edit, the changes that made it work: the wheel one and a half times larger and cut
by the circle, only three spokes with a clear gap, the new spoke held in his hand well in
front of the gap, his body running straight down to the cut, no cloth under the wheel.

### Agoros, the version that held

Reached through a market woman in profile with jars, a frontal woman with fruit, an awning, a
shop front with a dark wall, and a door frame.

```
Motif: a woman at her market stall, greeting a customer who stands outside
the picture. She is shown from the knees up, her body turned
three-quarters and her head in profile facing right: open wavy hair to the
shoulders held by a ribbon, a draped chiton fastened at the shoulder, a
warm smile. One hand is raised and open toward the right in a gesture of
welcome; the other rests on her hip. One sturdy wooden post stands to the
right of her and carries a slender bracket arm at its top, from which a
small rectangular signboard hangs on two short chains. The board has a
narrow border and two or three short strokes that suggest an inscription;
no letters. To the right of the post stands a market table with a cloth
draped over it that hangs down in heavy folds and is cut by the circle. On
the table stand four simple wares: a jug, a pair of sandals, a round loaf
of bread, and a rolled bolt of cloth. No door, no wall, no awning. Accent:
the signboard, her hair ribbon, and her shoulder clasp in bronze.
```

### Naos, the version that held

Reached through a temple portal, a column of drums, the builder with a temple model, and a
mosaic. The first wall had the builder behind the wall and the block floating in front of it;
the edit below fixed that by putting the block into the top course.

```
Motif: a Greek master builder setting a block onto a wall of dressed
stone. A mature man as a half-figure in profile facing right, cut by the
circle at the waist: wavy hair, a full, neatly trimmed beard, a calm and
concentrated face, leaning slightly forward. He wears a cloak draped over
one shoulder and fastened with a round clasp; both arms are bare. A low
wall of large rectangular stone blocks, all exactly the same size, runs
across the bottom of the medallion, laid in courses with staggered joints
like Greek ashlar masonry; it is cut by the circle. With both hands he
sets one more block into the top course: it is as long and as high as the
other blocks, sits in line with that course directly beside the last
block laid, and is held from both ends with open fingers. No white hole
behind or beside the block. Accent: the block in his hands and his
shoulder clasp in bronze. All other blocks are navy.
```

### Naos, the master builder (not adopted)

A good drawing on the first try, set aside because a finished temple stands for architecture
as a whole and the product provides building blocks.

```
Motif: a Greek master builder studying the model of a temple. A mature man
as a half-figure in profile facing right, cut by the circle at the waist:
short hair, a full, neatly trimmed beard, a calm and concentrated face. He
wears a cloak draped over one shoulder and fastened with a round clasp,
his forward arm bare. On the open palm of his raised forward hand, at
chest height and well in front of his face, he holds a small model of a
Greek temple and looks at it. The model is about as wide as his head. His
other hand holds a pair of dividers pointing down at his side. Accent: the
temple model and the shoulder clasp in bronze. The dividers are navy.
```

## Edit prompts

Send the last image back and change little:

```
Keep the woman, her pose, her face, her hair with the ribbon, her garment,
both arms and hands, and the line style exactly as in the attached image.
Change only these things:

1. <one change, with where and how large>
2. <one change>

Bronze (#BC8A45): <the parts>. Nothing else is bronze.
```

When the arrangement changes but the figure should stay:

```
Use the attached image as the reference for the man and the line style:
keep his head, face, beard, hair, and the cloak with its bronze shoulder
clasp. Redraw his arms and the rest of the scene as follows.
```

When only the layout should shift:

```
Keep everything exactly as in the attached image. Change only the
arrangement:

1. Move the woman and the post to the left, so that the woman stands in
   the left third of the medallion.
2. The freed space on the right shows more of the stall: the table is
   about one and a half times as wide as now and runs on to the right
   until the circle cuts it.
```

## Small versions

The wording that produced a good small Palamedes from the full mark was short: "a variant for
small resolutions, e.g. 32 px, with less detail that keeps the form". The sheet prompt below
says the same for all marks at once and adds what made that result work.

```
The attached sheet shows eight emblems of one product family. Draw the same
sheet again, in the same 4 x 2 arrangement and order, as small versions of
these emblems for 32 to 64 pixels. Each emblem keeps its figure, pose,
composition, objects, and colors, so that it is clearly the same mark.
Reduce the detail in the same way for all eight: draw hair as a few large
shapes, drop the fold lines in garments and the strands in hair, keep only
the most important inner lines, and make the outlines of faces, hands, and
objects bolder. Keep the bronze parts and make them a little larger rather
than smaller. Leave out the empty cream ring inside the bronze ring: the
motif reaches the bronze ring directly. Same bronze ring on all eight. No
new elements, no text, no labels.
```

For one mark, attach it alone and replace the first two sentences with "Make a variant of the
attached emblem for small sizes, 32 to 64 pixels."

What to expect: the model redraws rather than simplifies, so faces and hands come back a little
different. Lettering on objects disappears, which is right at these sizes. A supporting hand or
other minor part may turn into a blob; at 64 pixels and below that does not show.

## What went wrong, and the wording that fixed it

| What came back                         | Cause                                             | Wording that fixed it                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Every motif on the same stone pedestal | "stands on a base", and four emblems on one sheet | One emblem per run; "no pedestal, no floor"; the base is the figure's own garment or the ground |
| Pictogram-like, coarse shapes          | "simple enough to read at the size of a coin"     | Attach the reference; "richly articulated inside by fine cream lines"; "not a pictogram"        |
| A trumpet that reads as a megaphone    | The object alone, shown large                     | The figure who uses it                                                                          |
| Two garments merging into one body     | "their garments form one broad navy base"         | "Two separate garments... the figures touch only at the hands"                                  |
| A torso narrower than the arm          | No word on the body                               | "A broader, more powerful torso... his body is clearly wider than his upper arm"                |
| A waist that pinches and flares        | Garment left to the model                         | "His back runs straight down... the garment falls straight and does not flare out"              |
| A man who only holds a finished wheel  | The new part already in place                     | "The left spoke is missing... he holds one new spoke well in front of the gap"                  |
| A hull that reads as a brick wall      | A close, angled view of part of a ship            | The whole thing in strict side view; long planks "not bricks, no short vertical joints"         |
| A ship's bow that reads as a fish      | A large naturalistic eye on a pointed hull        | "A small ornamental eye... almond-shaped outline with a round pupil"                            |
| A woman who looks past her customer    | Profile, with the wares beside her                | A welcoming hand held out toward someone outside the picture                                    |
| A stern, priestly woman                | Headcloth and strict symmetry                     | Open wavy hair with a ribbon; "a clear, open smile"; head tilted slightly                       |
| A dark disc with cut-out openings      | "The wall of the shop is navy"                    | "No wall surfaces and no filled dark areas"; the architecture only hinted at, then dropped      |
| A sign that reads as a shutter box     | A board mounted flat above the window             | "Hangs on two short chains from a slender bracket arm... clearly a hanging sign"                |
| A bank portal                          | Two columns and a doorway from the front          | No wording fixed it; the motif was dropped                                                      |
| A lighthouse                           | One column on a mound with a bronze drum above it | No wording fixed it; the motif was dropped                                                      |
