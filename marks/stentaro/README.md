# Stentaro

**Product:** a headless publisher for social media posts.

## The idea

The name comes from Stentor, the herald in the Iliad whose voice carries like that of fifty
men. A herald does not speak for himself: he makes sure that what others have to say is heard.
That is what a publisher does.

## The mark

Stentor as a bust in profile, head tilted back, sounding a salpinx, the long straight trumpet
of Greek heralds.

- **Navy:** hair, cloak, and the raised arm. Face and hand are paper color with navy contours,
  which sets him apart from the dark Palamedes head.
- **Bronze:** the salpinx, the thing that carries the voice.

## What was tried and dropped

- **The salpinx alone, on a speaker's platform:** the trumpet was the smallest part of the
  picture.
- **The salpinx alone, large and cut by the ring:** it read as a megaphone. What Stentor is
  about, someone who calls, was missing.

The lesson for the family: a bare object did not carry a mark; the figure using it did.

## Placement

Taken over as drawn, scaled to 94% so that hair and trumpet stay clear of the paper ring.
