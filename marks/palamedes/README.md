# Palamedes

**Product:** translation and localization tooling for web applications.

## The idea

Palamedes is the hero to whom the Greeks ascribed the invention of letters, numbers, and
counting. A product that carries text from one language into another is named after the man
who, in the story, gave the Greeks writing in the first place.

## The mark

A young man's head in profile with a laurel wreath, cut at the neck like a bust on a coin.

- **Navy:** the head, drawn as one dark silhouette with hair strands in paper color.
- **Bronze:** the laurel, the mark of the one who is honored for an invention.

## History

- This was the first mark of the family, drawn before the ring frame existed. Its ring was 6
  units wide and the bust ended freely inside it.
- In November 2026 the head was moved onto the frame unchanged: ring width 10, an empty paper
  ring, the bust lowered so that it is cut on the window.
