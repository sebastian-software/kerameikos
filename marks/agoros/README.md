# Agoros

**Product:** websites for small local businesses; in the German market it runs under the name
Vorort. The mark is meant to carry under either name.

## The idea

The agora was the marketplace of a Greek town: the place where traders and craftspeople had
their stalls and where people found them. A website does for a small business what a stall and
its sign did there: it is the place where customers find you and recognize you.

"Agoros" is a coined name; the Greek word is _agorá_ (ἀγορά).

## The mark

A woman at her market stall, turned toward a customer outside the picture. One hand is held
out in welcome, the other rests on her hip. Beside her stands a post with a hanging shop sign,
and in front of it a table with a draped cloth and four wares.

- **Navy:** her hair and garment, the post, the table with its cloth, and the wares.
- **Paper color:** face, arms, and hands. There are no wall surfaces; the scene stands free on
  paper.
- **Bronze:** the shop sign, her hair ribbon, and her shoulder clasp.

Three decisions should survive any redrawing:

- **The sign is the accent.** It is what the product gives a business: the board by which it is
  found and known. It hangs free and carries strokes instead of letters.
- **The wares are mixed:** a jug, sandals, bread, cloth. The product is not for one trade.
- **The architecture is left out.** A door, a wall, and an awning were all tried and made the
  mark heavy or unclear. Stall and sign are enough.

## What was tried and dropped

- **The earlier mark, a map pin above fields:** a pictogram from maps that says "location", not
  "business".
- **A market woman in profile holding up a jug, with jars on a counter:** read as pottery or
  wine, and she looked past her customer.
- **The same woman from the front behind a heap of fruit:** the only frontal face in the
  family. It showed a fruit seller, which the product is not for, and it looked like the label
  of a food brand; without a stall she could as well be a mother handing over fruit.
- **The frontal woman under a striped awning:** modern, crowded, and the figure became the
  smallest in the family.
- **A shop front with a dark wall, a doorway, and a counter window:** too much navy, a door
  shorter than the woman, and a sign that read as a shutter box.
- **The doorway as a thin frame:** flimsy beside the other marks, and it said nothing that
  stall and sign do not say.

## Known limit

This is a narrative mark with small parts. At 32 pixels it shows the figure, the post, and the
sign as a light bar; the wares blur.

## Placement

Taken over as drawn, scaled to 91% and moved 4 units right and 3 units down, so that her elbow
on the left stays inside the window. The motif is wide, so her head ends at y = 51, below the
top limit; the post rises a little higher, as a narrow element may.
